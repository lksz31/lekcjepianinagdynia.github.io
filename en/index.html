About Me

I'm a piano teacher based in Gdynia. I graduated from the Zygmunt Noskowski State Music School (1st and 2nd degree) in Gdynia, and I play piano and oboe professionally. I've been teaching piano for almost two years, alongside group robotics classes for children aged 8–10, and I also work as a certified camp counsellor.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a piano lesson in Gdynia cost?

A one-hour lesson at the studio in Gdynia (Mały Kack) costs 119 PLN. For lessons at the student's home without their own piano, an instrument-delivery fee applies (+19 PLN for a Roland GO:PIANO88PX or +49 PLN for a Yamaha P-525).

From what age can a child start learning piano?

Lessons can start from age 6. The youngest students learn using color- and picture-based methods instead of standard notation at first.

Do I need to own a piano to start?

No. Lessons can take place at the studio in Gdynia, where an instrument is available, or at your home — if there's no piano yet, one can be brought along. Early lessons can also work on a keyboard if that's what you have at home.

Are keyboard lessons available too?

Yes. Lessons use a hybrid piano that combines the classic sound and key mechanics of an acoustic instrument with the flexibility of a digital one, so skills transfer freely between piano and keyboard.

Do you teach adults?

Yes — lessons are available for both children and adults, including complete beginners and people returning to the piano after years away.

Where do lessons take place?

Lessons take place at the studio in Gdynia (Mały Kack), or at your home in Gdynia, Sopot and the surrounding area.
